Send to Odysseus

Captures (right-click → Send to Odysseus, Alt+Shift+O, or the SpecTracer quick action) post to your Odysseus Notes. If the send fails, the capture is copied to the clipboard instead.

The extension has access to http://127.0.0.1:7000 and http://localhost:7000.

Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local). Never committed to the repo.
